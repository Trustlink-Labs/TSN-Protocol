BULLETIN 02 — THE PROBLEM

Payments have no identity.

Crypto made money programmable — but paying someone still means copying a 40-character address and hoping it is right.

There is no portable identity, no privacy boundary, no liability standard. Every payment leaks who paid, who received, and how much.

The rules of money were settled over centuries: identity, privacy, settlement. Crypto re-opened the question and answered it with a string of characters.

Lost moneyOne wrong character and funds are gone. Address poisoning steals from wallets every year.
Lost privacySender, recipient and balance metadata sit on-chain for anyone to read.
Lost timeNo labels, no routing, no safety net — every payment is copy, paste, and pray.
Missed mainstreamNormal users will not send money to a random string of characters.

BULLETIN 07 — CROSS-CHAIN PROTOCOL

Attestcoin: proof-verified
settlement.

TSN's corridors are secured by Attestcoin proof verification. TSN deploys an Attestcoin Smart Contract (ASC) on each destination chain. Before any cross-chain payout is authorized, the ASC verifies proof-backed liquidity events using the chain's native Block Prover precompile — Creditcoin is corridor one; every future EVM corridor uses the same path.
